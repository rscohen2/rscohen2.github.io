Questions I’m working on

What do language models learn about culture?

LLMs are trained on centuries of writing. I look at which cultural patterns they pick up, which they flatten, and whose voices get lost along the way.

How does culture change, and where does it start?

Using computational methods on large text collections, I trace how new ideas and styles emerge and spread through literature and media.

Who gets to be an author?

From global bestseller lists to AI-generated text, I study how markets, brands, and machines are changing what authorship means.
